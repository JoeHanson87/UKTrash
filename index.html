About UK Trash

With over 20 years collective history in the waste management industry, UK Trash is committed to recycling 91% of all waste removed.

We use software to issue digital waste collection receipts (waste transfer notes) for every job, giving you clear and accessible documentation. Customers are notified of expected collection times, helping you plan your day more efficiently. Need a collection? Simply send a request through our convenient online form.

With our transparent and streamlined approach, you'll always know where your waste is going, giving you peace of mind that it's handled responsibly. Together, we're reducing fly-tipping, supporting sustainability, and making waste management easier than ever.

Our Services

From business waste to emergency clearances and jet washing, we cover it all.

Eco Chemical Clean

Once your waste has been removed, UK Trash offer an industrial jet washing chemical clean service. We can jet wash a range of surfaces including driveways, paths, patios, car parks and most hardened plastics areas. We always go that extra mile to help our customers maintain their homes and businesses in the neatest, cleanest way possible.

Wheelie bin cleans – weekly, fortnightly or monthly. This service is offered free of charge if we supply your business waste management. One-off or regular community visits are also available, charged per bin.

Wheelie Bins

We supply bins from 75 litres to 1100 litres for businesses and homes, complete with a fitted lock system to protect you from fly-tippers. Only you and UK Trash operatives will have access to your bins.

  • Collections available daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly
  • You only pay for each collection – no bin rental fees
  • All bins cleaned free of charge when needed
  • Lightweight 1100 litre bins with lockable wheels, ideal for spring cleans and garage clearances
Skip Bags

All sizes available – we drop off and collect when filled. A skip bag is a large durable waterproof bag that can be filled with waste and taken away.

It is a convenient and lightweight alternative to traditional metal skips for household or garden clearances, home renovations and DIY projects. Skip bags are made from ultra-strong woven plastic and can hold up to 1000–1500kg.

Industrial Jet Washing

Our industrial jet washing service uses the power of high-pressure water to clean surfaces on commercial and residential buildings in a way that may not be possible with even the strongest chemicals.

A jet wash utilises a high-pressure, narrow jet of cold or hot water to break dirt free from driveways, patios, car parks and more.

Recycling

We aim to recycle 91% of all waste.

We research all waste transfer stations used by UK Trash and carry out the necessary checks on waste studies to make sure we are using the best in the business in every area of our work.

We donate furniture, clothes and white goods, working alongside charities to provide transport, deliver or supply used goods and furniture to those in need. If it's good to go, it's getting recycled or upcycled – that's a fact. We maintain a database of goods needed, and the minute items arrive in good working order we clean them up and drop them off to the store or directly to the chosen donation address.
